# User Manual — AI Financial Advisor Platform

Welcome to **AI Financial Advisor** — your intelligent, AI-powered personal wealth and prediction ecosystem.

---

## 1. Quick Start & Access

1. Open the application in your browser (default `http://localhost:8501`).
2. Use one of the pre-configured demo personas on the **Login** page:

---

## 2. Navigating Core Features

### 📊 Dashboard
- Monitor your real-time **Total Balance**, **Monthly Inflow**, **Expenses**, and **Net Savings Rate**.
- Review dynamic **AI Financial Insights** highlighting spending anomalies and budget adherence.
- View interactive **Plotly Cash Flow** bar charts and **Category Donuts**.

### 💳 Transactions & Income
- **Add Transactions**: Input description and amount; the NLP model automatically predicts the category and checks for anomalies.
- **Search & Filter**: Search by merchant or filter by date range and transaction type.
- **Export Ledger**: Download clean CSV records anytime.

### 📷 Receipt Scanner (Computer Vision)
- Drag and drop any receipt image (JPG, PNG, PDF).
- OpenCV image preprocessing enhances text edges and runs OCR entity extraction.
- Verify parsed line items, tax, and totals, then click **Create Verified Transaction Record** to save.

### 🧠 AI Budget Planner
- Get intelligent monthly budget limits based on the **50/30/20 rule** customized to your actual spending.
- Track real-time category progress bars with overspending warning alerts.

### 📈 Financial Forecast (Deep Learning)
- Review 30-day forward spending projections generated by the **LSTM Recurrent Neural Network**.
- Inspect upper and lower 90% confidence ribbon bands to plan for upcoming cashflow requirements.

### 🛡️ Fraud & Anomaly Sandbox
- Simulate transactions with varying amounts, times of day (e.g. 3 AM), and merchant novelty.
- Inspect the **Isolation Forest Anomaly Score** and plain-English explanation factors.

### 🎯 Credit Risk & Explainable AI (XAI)
- Adjust your financial metrics (income, debt, credit utilization, payment history).
- View your estimated credit tier, default probability, and transparent **Feature Importance Bar Charts**.

### 💎 Investment Advisor & Portfolio
- Take the automated Risk Profiler questionnaire to classify your investor archetype (**Conservative**, **Moderate**, **Aggressive**).
- Track live holdings, unrealized capital gains/losses, and portfolio concentration risk.

### 💬 AI Chatbot Assistant
- Ask natural language questions like *"Where am I spending the most?"*, *"How much did I spend this month?"*, or *"Analyze my financial health"*.
- The assistant retrieves your personal MongoDB metrics and crafts contextual advice.

### 📑 Executive Report Generator
- Generate professional audit-ready financial statements in **PDF**, **Excel**, or **CSV** with one click.
